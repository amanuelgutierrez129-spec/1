About DMA-axi

Professional Financial Consultancy Established in Durham UK

Founded with core focus on global financial information consulting, DMA-axi is registered and operated from Durham, England. We gather real-time global financial market data, analyze industry trends and provide tailored investment consultation for corporate and private clients across Europe and worldwide regions.

Our core philosophy is data-driven decision support, combining experienced financial analysts and market experts to help customers avoid investment risks and optimize asset allocation with systematic consulting solutions.

  • Registered and regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) with license number: FRN 897654
  • Headquartered in Durham with satellite offices in London, Frankfurt and New York
  • Specialized in cross-border financial services for SMEs and high-net-worth individuals
  • ISO 9001 certified for financial consulting service quality

We adhere to the highest ethical standards and regulatory compliance, ensuring all our consulting services are transparent, objective and aligned with client's long-term financial goals.

Our Core Services

01

Global Financial Market Research

Collect and analyze real-time global stock, forex and commodity market data, deliver periodic market research reports with detailed trend predictions and risk evaluation.

Key Deliverables: Weekly market briefings, monthly in-depth analysis reports, quarterly outlook forecasts, ad-hoc market event analysis. Our research covers 45+ global markets and 120+ industry sectors.
02

Personalized Investment Consulting

Custom asset allocation plans based on client’s capital scale, risk preference and investment cycle, one-on-one professional financial advisor guidance throughout investment cycles.

Service Process: Initial financial health assessment → Risk profiling → Custom portfolio design → Regular performance review → Tactical adjustments as market conditions change. Minimum investment advisory threshold: £50,000.
03

Enterprise Financial Advisory

Provide mid & small-sized companies with financing planning, cross-border investment consulting and industry financial feasibility analysis reports for business expansion.

Specializations: Cross-border trade finance, EU market entry financial planning, SME grant application support, export/import financial risk management.
04

Financial Data Analytics

Process massive financial raw data via professional modeling, visualize market changes and extract valuable business insights for investment reference.

Tools & Methodologies: Advanced statistical modeling, machine learning algorithms, predictive analytics, interactive data visualization dashboards. We integrate data from Bloomberg, Reuters and proprietary data sources.
05

Risk Management Consulting

Identify potential financial risks for individual and institutional clients, design effective risk hedging plans to control investment loss within expected range.

Risk Areas Covered: Market risk, credit risk, liquidity risk, currency risk, regulatory risk. We offer stress testing, scenario analysis and risk mitigation strategy development.
06

Cross-border Finance Guide

Offer cross-border fund flow consultation, overseas investment policy interpretation and international financial regulation consulting for global asset layout.

Regulatory Expertise: UK FCA, EU MiFID II, US SEC, offshore financial regulations. We assist with cross-border tax optimization, compliance documentation and regulatory reporting.
